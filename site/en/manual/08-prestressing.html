Chapter 8 Prestressing

keta represents tendon prestressing by the equivalent load method: the force of each tendon, after friction and anchorage draw-in, is converted into anchorage forces, deviation forces and friction forces applied to the bars it passes through. On a statically determinate structure, these loads reproduce N = −P and M = −P·e; on a statically indeterminate structure, the difference is the secondary (hyperstatic) prestress. Three tools are available:

  • the tendons of the bars, stressed during a staged construction analysis (Sections 8.1 to 8.3);
  • the unit stressing cases of a bar, for the adjustment of stay cables or ties (Section 8.4);
  • the tendons embedded in shells (Section 8.8).

8.1 Definition of a tendon#

Syntax
m.add_tendon(bars, P0, profile, jack='start', mu=0.19, k=0.01, slip=0.006, Ep=195e9, Ap=0.0,
             fpk=0.0, rho1000=2.5, relaxation_class=2, s_begin=0.0, s_end=-1.0, name='')  →  int
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
bars——ordered list of the bars passed through (continuous chain)
P0N—stressing force at the live anchorage
profilem—tendon profile: list of points (s, e_y, e_z), s curvilinear abscissa from the origin of the chain, e_y and e_z eccentricities in the local axes of each bar; linear interpolation between the points; a single point gives a straight profile
jack—'start'live anchorage: 'start' (origin of the chain), 'end' (end), 'both' (both)
mu1/rad0.19coefficient of friction in curves
krad/m0.01unintentional angular deviation per unit length
slipm0.006anchorage draw-in (wedge set)
EpPa195e9modulus of the prestressing steel
Apm²0steel area; 0: tendon defined by its force alone (no losses by shortening and no time-dependent losses)
fpkPa0characteristic strength; 0: no relaxation
rho1000%2.5relaxation loss at 1000 hours
relaxation_class—2relaxation class (1, 2 or 3, EN 1992-1-1 clause 3.3.2)
s_begin, s_endm0; −1abscissae of the anchorages on the chain (−1: end of the chain)
name—''name of the tendon

The force along the tendon follows the friction law

P(x) = P₀ · e−μ (θ(x) + k·x)

where θ(x) is the cumulative angular deviation from the live anchorage and x the distance travelled. Anchorage draw-in is treated by the mirror rule; with two live anchorages, the force retained is the envelope of the two curves.

Important

The parameter k is an unintentional angular deviation per metre, multiplied by μ in the exponent. A rule that gives the linear loss directly (loss in e−φ·x) corresponds to k = φ / μ.

Note

As the profile is piecewise linear, the deviation forces are concentrated at the points of the profile. A curved profile is described by a sufficient number of points (for example one point every metre or every 1/40 of the span, as in the example below).

8.2 Stressing#

A tendon acts when a stage of the staged construction analysis stresses it, through the key tension of the dictionary of the stage (Chapter 13):

dict(name="mise en tension", time=28, tension=[dict(tendon=c1), dict(tendon=c2, fraction=0.5)])
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
tendon——identifier of the tendon
fraction—1cumulative fraction of P₀ aimed at after the stage; a value lower than the fraction already applied destresses the tendon (negative value: complete destressing)
mode—'jack''jack': stressing against the concrete; 'bed': stressing on a bed (pretensioning, no force on the structure); 'release': release of the bed and transfer by bond
unbonded—Falseungrouted tendon (sliding in its duct) until its grouting by the stage key inject
group—0stressing group: the tendons of the same group are stressed simultaneously, successive groups cause elastic losses to one another

The immediate losses by elastic shortening of the concrete, then the time-dependent losses by shrinkage, creep and relaxation (EN 1992-1-1 clause 5.10.6, applied incrementally between the dates of the stages), are computed automatically when Ap is non-zero. The result of each stage gives, for each tendon, the quadruple (identifier, stressing force, cumulative losses, effective force) through phase.tendons().

Note

To obtain the effect of prestressing alone in a static analysis without creep, a staged construction analysis with a single stage on a material without time-dependent law is used.

8.3 Example: prestressed beam#

A statically determinate 30 m concrete beam (EC2 law) is prestressed by a parabolic tendon of 3000 mm² stressed to 0.75 fpk from the origin. Self-weight and prestressing act at 28 days; the losses, the deflection and the stresses at midspan are followed up to 30 years.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_precontrainte.py
# Prestressing by tendon: simply supported concrete beam of 30 m (EC2), parabolic
# tendon stressed at 28 days from the start end; final state at 30 years.
import keta

L, nb = 30.0, 12
m = keta.Model()
concrete = m.add_material(e=35e9, nu=0.2, rho=2500.0)
m.set_concrete_law(concrete, "EC2", fc=40e6, rh=70.0, h_mm=480.0, cement="N")
b_, h_ = 0.6, 1.6
sec = m.add_section_rect(b=b_, h=h_)
n = [m.add_node(L * i / nb, 0, 0) for i in range(nb + 1)]
bars = [m.add_beam(n[i], n[i + 1], sec, concrete) for i in range(nb)]
m.add_support(n[0], dx=True, dy=True, dz=True, rx=True)
m.add_support(n[-1], dy=True, dz=True)
m.add_self_weight(case=1)
m.name_case(1, "self-weight")

# parabolic profile: e_z = 0 at the anchorages, -0.60 m at midspan (below the centroid)
profile = [(s, 0.0, -0.60 * 4 * s * (L - s) / L**2) for s in [L * i / 40 for i in range(41)]]
Ap, fpk = 3.0e-3, 1860e6
tendon = m.add_tendon(bars, P0=0.75 * fpk * Ap, profile=profile, jack="start",
                      mu=0.19, k=0.005, slip=0.006, Ep=195e9, Ap=Ap, fpk=fpk, rho1000=2.5,
                      relaxation_class=2, name="C1")

r = m.phased([
    dict(name="stressing", time=28, cases=[1], tension=[dict(tendon=tendon)]),
    dict(name="entry in service", time=90),
    dict(name="30 years", time=28 + 30 * 365),
], creep_along_bar=True)

A, I = b_ * h_, b_ * h_**3 / 12
mid = nb // 2
for i in range(len(r)):
    p = r.phase(i)
    tid, jacked, loss, effective = p.tendons()[0]
    x, N, Vy, Vz, T, My, Mz = p.bar_forces(bars[mid])[0]           # midspan section
    s_bot = N / A + My * (-h_ / 2) / I                              # bottom fibre
    s_top = N / A + My * (h_ / 2) / I                               # top fibre
    print(f"{p.name:16}: P = {jacked / 1e6:.3f} MN, losses = {loss / 1e6:.3f} MN, "
          f"effective = {effective / 1e6:.3f} MN")
    print(f"{'':16}  deflection = {p.displacement(n[mid])[2] * 1e3:7.2f} mm, "
          f"sigma bot = {s_bot / 1e6:6.2f} MPa, sigma top = {s_top / 1e6:6.2f} MPa")

# Statically determinate check at stressing: My = -q L²/8 - P(L/2) e_z(L/2), P(L/2) = -N
p = r.phase(0)
x, N, Vy, Vz, T, My, Mz = p.bar_forces(bars[mid])[0]
q = 2500.0 * 9.80665 * A
print(f"midspan: P(L/2) = {-N / 1e6:.4f} MN, My = {My / 1e3:.2f} kN.m, "
      f"-qL²/8 + 0.60 P = {(-q * L**2 / 8 - N * 0.60) / 1e3:.2f} kN.m")
Output
stressing       : P = 4.185 MN, losses = 0.000 MN, effective = 4.185 MN
                  deflection =   -3.56 mm, sigma bot =  -3.10 MPa, sigma top =  -5.17 MPa
entry in service: P = 4.185 MN, losses = 0.098 MN, effective = 4.087 MN
                  deflection =   -6.72 mm, sigma bot =  -2.78 MPa, sigma top =  -5.29 MPa
30 years        : P = 4.185 MN, losses = 0.314 MN, effective = 3.871 MN
                  deflection =  -12.66 mm, sigma bot =  -2.09 MPa, sigma top =  -5.56 MPa
midspan: P(L/2) = 3.9707 MN, My = -265.37 kN.m, -qL²/8 + 0.60 P = -265.37 kN.m

The losses displayed are the immediate and time-dependent losses; the loss by friction and by anchorage draw-in is contained in the force profile: at midspan, the force is 3.971 MN against 4.185 MN at the anchorage. The last line checks the statically determinate identity My = −qL²/8 − P e_z.

8.4 Unit stressing cases and adjustment#

Syntax
m.add_unit_prestress_case(bar, P=1.0)  →  int
m.tune_linear(base, unknowns, targets)  →  dict

add_unit_prestress_case creates a new load case in which the bar bar (stay cable, tie, straight tendon modelled by a bar) is stressed with a force P where it could shorten freely: this is an imposed strain −P/(EA). The method returns the number of the case created. In the structure, the resulting force is lower than P, the structure deforming under the effect of the tension.

tune_linear seeks the coefficients xj of the unknown cases such that the combination “base case + Σ xj · casej” reaches target values. The problem is solved exactly (convex quadratic program):

ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
base——list of the case numbers of the base combination (coefficient 1)
unknowns——list of the cases whose coefficient is sought: a number, or dict(case, lower, upper, cost, ref) with bounds, cost and reference value
targets——list of targets dict(kind, id, comp, value, mode, weight, station)

A target applies to a displacement (kind='displacement', id = node), a reaction ('reaction', node), a bar force ('bar_force', id = bar, station = index of the output section) or a link force ('link_force'); comp is the component (0 to 5). The mode is 'equal' (equality, default), 'at_most', 'at_least' (inequalities) or 'fit' (least squares weighted by weight). The result is a dictionary x (coefficients), achieved (values obtained for the targets) and base (values of the targets under the base case alone).

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_reglage.py
# Stay tuning: two stays supporting a cantilever beam of 20 m; the stay
# tensions that bring the deflection of the two attachment points back to zero are sought.
import keta

m = keta.Model()
steel = m.steel()
foot, head = m.add_node(0, 0, 0), m.add_node(0, 0, 12)
p = [m.add_node(5.0 * i, 0, 0) for i in range(1, 5)]          # x = 5, 10, 15, 20 m
deck = m.add_section_props(sx=0.05, iy=0.02, iz=0.2, ix=0.01)
pylon = m.add_section_props(sx=0.2, iy=0.5, iz=0.5, ix=0.3)
m.add_beam(foot, p[0], deck, steel)
for i in range(3):
    m.add_beam(p[i], p[i + 1], deck, steel)
m.add_beam(foot, head, pylon, steel)
stay = m.add_section_props(sx=4e-3, iy=1e-9, iz=1e-9, ix=1e-9)
h1 = m.add_truss(head, p[1], stay, steel)                      # attachment at 10 m
h2 = m.add_truss(head, p[3], stay, steel)                      # attachment at 20 m
m.fix(foot)
for b in (1, 2, 3, 4):
    m.add_udl(b, wz=-80e3, case=1)                             # deck weight
m.name_case(1, "permanent")
c1 = m.add_unit_prestress_case(h1, P=1.0)                      # unit tension cases
c2 = m.add_unit_prestress_case(h2, P=1.0)

targets = [dict(kind="displacement", id=p[1], comp=2, value=0.0),
           dict(kind="displacement", id=p[3], comp=2, value=0.0)]
t = m.tune_linear(base=[1], unknowns=[c1, c2], targets=targets)
print("tuning tensions [kN]:           ", [round(x / 1e3, 3) for x in t["x"]])
print("deflections obtained [mm]:      ", [round(x * 1e3, 9) + 0.0 for x in t["achieved"]])
print("deflections without tuning [mm]:", [round(x * 1e3, 3) for x in t["base"]])

# check: combination of the permanent case and the weighted unit cases
cmb = m.add_combination([(1.0, 1), (t["x"][0], c1), (t["x"][1], c2)], name="tuned")
res = m.analyze().combo(cmb)
print(f"combination: uz(10 m) = {res.displacement(p[1])[2] * 1e3:.6f} mm, "
      f"N stay 1 = {res.bar_forces(h1)[0][1] / 1e3:.3f} kN, N stay 2 = {res.bar_forces(h2)[0][1] / 1e3:.3f} kN")
Output
tuning tensions [kN]:            [1489.599, 888.402]
deflections obtained [mm]:       [0.0, 0.0]
deflections without tuning [mm]: [-22.327, -49.798]
combination: uz(10 m) = 0.000000 mm, N stay 1 = 1190.133 kN, N stay 2 = 610.862 kN

The coefficients are the “free bar” tensions to be introduced by the unit cases; the forces actually present in the stay cables under the adjusted combination (1190 and 611 kN) are lower, part of the imposed elongation being absorbed by the deformation of the structure.

8.5 General form finding#

Syntax
m.tune_cables(case, unknowns, targets, tol=1e-9, max_iter=50, substeps=1,
               large_displacements=False)  →  dict
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
case——load case of the state sought (self-weight, permanent loads)
unknowns——unstrained lengths of cables (cable number, or dict(cable=…, lower, upper, cost, ref)) and length defects of bars (dict(bar=…, …): bar fabricated too short by x, imposed strain −x/L)
targets——targets dict(kind, id, comp, value, mode, weight, station), as for tune_linear, plus 'cable_tension' (id = cable, comp 0 or 1: the end)
tol—1e-9relative tolerance; a displacement aimed at zero is reached to tol × the size of the structure
large_displacements—Falseframe in large displacements (without length defect)

The nonlinear problem (exact cable statics) is solved by successive quadratic programs on the finite-difference linearisation, until the equality targets are reached and, with fitted ('fit') or bounded targets, until the step has stabilised. Dependent equality targets (at least as many as unknowns, without cost) are refused by a message that names the targets involved and the unknowns they do not determine. The result gives x, achieved, iterations and the displacements of the state found.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_recherche_forme.py
# Form finding: simply supported beam of 20 m (20 kN/m) held at midspan by a stay at 45°;
# unstressed length of the stay that cancels the midspan deflection (vertical force 5 q L / 8), then the same
# by the length defect of a prop.
import math
import keta

m = keta.Model()
mat = m.add_material(e=2e11, nu=0.3, rho=0.0)
deck = m.add_section_props(sx=0.01, iy=0.04, iz=0.04, ix=0.01)           # EA = 2e9 N
n = [m.add_node(i * 1.0, 0, 0) for i in range(21)]
for i in range(20):
    m.add_udl(m.add_beam(n[i], n[i + 1], deck, mat), wz=-2e4, case=1)
m.add_support(n[0], dx=True, dy=True, dz=True, rx=True)
m.add_support(n[20], dy=True, dz=True, rx=True)
pylon = m.add_node(0, 0, 10)
m.fix(pylon)
stay = m.add_cable(pylon, n[10], ea=1e8, w=0.0, h=1e5)                   # unstressed length: the unknown

r = m.tune_cables(1, [stay], [dict(id=n[10], comp=2, value=0.0)], tol=1e-10)
F = 5 * 2e4 * 20 / 8
H = F / (1 + F / 2e9)
print(f"L0 of the stay {r['x'][0]:.6f} m in {r['iterations']} iterations; midspan deflection "
      f"{r['displacement'][n[10]][2] * 1e3:.2e} mm")
print(f"by hand: vertical force 5qL/8 = {F / 1e3:.1f} kN, tension {math.hypot(H, F) / 1e3:.2f} kN")
Output
L0 of the stay 14.091434 m in 2 iterations; midspan deflection 2.01e-08 mm
by hand: vertical force 5qL/8 = 250.0 kN, tension 353.53 kN

The stay cable carries the vertical force 5qL/8 that cancels the deflection of a simply supported beam; its horizontal component compresses the half-deck, which shortens, and the unstrained length found allows for this shortening.

8.6 Fabrication camber#

Syntax
m.deck_camber(case, targets, max_iterations=30, tol=1e-9)  →  dict

Seeks the vertical fabrication offsets of the nodes of targets ({node: target level z after the permanent case}) such that the deformed shape under the case case reaches these levels. Each pass refabricates the structure (node levels shifted, the bars follow) and recomputes it — cable statics if the model carries cables, linear statics otherwise — then corrects c ← c − (z − target). The result gives camber (offset per node, positive upwards), deviation, iterations and trace (maximum deviation at each pass). A node restrained vertically cannot be targeted.

For a simply supported beam to be brought level under its self-weight, the camber is the deflection of the undeformed beam, qx(L³ − 2Lx² + x³)/(24EI), up to the second-order effect of the slopes; two passes are enough.

8.7 Suspension: form finding of the main cable#

form_find_cable seeks the shape of a main cable each segment of which, between two hangers, is an elastic catenary in equilibrium at its nodes under the hanger loads. It returns the horizontal tension H, the nodes, the unstrained length and the maximum tension of each segment, the force of each hanger, the sag achieved, the forces on the saddles and the total unstrained length. For a weightless cable under equal, evenly spaced loads, the shape is the funicular polygon of the distributed load: H = q L² / (8 f).

m.build_suspension performs this form finding with the stiffness and the weight of the material and section of the cable, then assembles the result in the model: the cable nodes, one cable per segment at its unstrained length, one hanger from each cable node to the deck node. It returns form (the result of the form finding), cable_nodes, cables and backstays (cable identifiers in the model), hanger_bars, hanger_l0 and anchor_nodes. The equilibrium is then computed by m.cable_static.

A hanger with tension T elongates by T L / EA. Placed without stress at its geometric length, it lets the deck move down by this elongation under the permanent load. hanger_fit_case designates the case that receives the length defect of each hanger, as an imposed strain −(L − L0) / L0 on its bar. The hanger then carries its form-finding tension in the drawn geometry, and the deck stays at its level when the loads given to the hangers are those it transmits to them. A continuous deck whose reactions are not these loads keeps the deflection of this redistribution. hanger_l0 gives the unstrained length L0 of each hanger, to be fabricated: L0 = L / (1 + T / EA) for a weightless hanger. keta.hanger_unstrained_length gives it for a tension at one end and a weight: L = L0 + (T L0 + w L0² / 2) / EA, with w_axial = w from the foot of a vertical hanger (the tension increases upwards) and −w from its head. An inclined hanger carries w × height difference / L along its axis; the sag due to its self-weight is not modelled. The hangers are not grouped by fabrication lengths.

m.solve_uelm adds the second step: on the assembled structure, the deck takes part of the permanent load and the cable leaves its profile; the unstrained lengths of the segments are corrected until each hanger node recovers the level of the profile of the first step. The result adds l0 (corrected lengths, carried by the cables of the model), deviation_stage1 and deviation (deviation before and after correction) and iterations. The model must not already carry cables. The hangers are assembled at their unstrained length, their length defect carried by the case case; the same applies on flexible towers.

m.build_suspension_on_towers places the saddles on flexible towers: left_node and right_node are the tower-top nodes, already in the model. The shape of the cable is sought with the saddles where the tower tops are under the permanent case case, by fixed-point iteration between the form finding and the analysis of the whole, until the saddles no longer move by more than saddle_tol. The result adds saddle_disp_left, saddle_disp_right (displacements of the tower tops under the permanent case), saddle_gap and iterations. Without backstay, a tower top moves towards the span by H / k, k being the lateral stiffness of the tower.

m.suspension_response returns the response of a suspension — the dict returned by one of the three assembly functions — under a case: cable_H and cable_Tmax (horizontal tension and maximum tension of the main cable), segment_H, hanger_tension, the extreme moments deck_my_max, deck_my_min and the extreme vertical displacements deck_dz_min, deck_dz_max of the deck (bars deck_bars), backstay_H and backstay_Tmax. m.suspension_live_response gives the effect of a live load: superposition does not hold for cables, so the permanent case alone and the two loads together are both computed. The result carries dead, total and the differences hanger_delta, cable_H_delta, deck_my_max_delta, deck_my_min_delta.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_suspension.py
# Suspension bridge: a deck of 21 spans of 10 m hung from a main cable between two saddles
# 240 m apart. Form finding of the cable under the hanger loads, assembly, then
# hangers at their unstressed length, two-stage correction of the unstressed lengths of the cable (the stiff
# deck carries part of the load).
import keta

def bridge():
    m = keta.Model()
    steel = m.add_material(200e9, 0.3, rho=0.0)
    wire = m.add_material(195e9, 0.3, rho=7850.0)
    rod = m.add_material(160e9, 0.3, rho=0.0)
    s_deck = m.add_section_props(0.5, 0.8, 5.0, 1.0)
    s_cable = m.add_section_props(0.30, 1e-6, 1e-6, 1e-6)
    s_hanger = m.add_section_props(0.01, 1e-8, 1e-8, 1e-8)
    nh, x0, dx, q = 21, 20.0, 10.0, 150e3
    nodes = [m.add_node(x0 - dx / 2, 0, 5.0)] + [m.add_node(x0 + dx * i, 0, 5.0) for i in range(nh)] \
        + [m.add_node(x0 + dx * (nh - 1) + dx / 2, 0, 5.0)]
    bars = [m.add_beam(a, b, s_deck, steel) for a, b in zip(nodes, nodes[1:])]
    m.add_support(nodes[0], dx=True, dy=True, rx=True)       # the deck is hung, not supported
    m.add_support(nodes[-1], dy=True)
    for b in bars:
        m.add_udl(b, wz=-q, case=1)
    data = dict(left=(0, 0, 40), right=(240, 0, 40), hanger_x=[x0 + dx * i for i in range(nh)],
                hanger_load=[q * dx] * nh, hanger_deck_nodes=nodes[1:-1], cable_material=wire,
                cable_section=s_cable, hanger_material=rod, hanger_section=s_hanger, target_sag=25.0)
    return m, data, q * dx * nh

# 1. form finding alone: horizontal tension and unstressed length of the cable
m, data, weight = bridge()
shape = keta.form_find_cable(data["left"], data["right"], data["hanger_x"], data["hanger_load"],
                             ea=195e9 * 0.30, w=7850.0 * 0.30 * 9.80665, target_sag=25.0)
print(f"shape: H = {shape['H'] / 1e6:.2f} MN, sag {shape['sag']:.3f} m, unstressed length {shape['total_l0']:.3f} m")

# 2. assembly in the model and equilibrium under the deck weight
sm = m.build_suspension(**data)
r = m.cable_static(case=1)
deviation = max(abs(r.displacement(n)[2]) for n in sm["cable_nodes"][1:-1])
print(f"assembled: {len(sm['cables'])} cable segments, {len(sm['hanger_bars'])} hangers; "
      f"vertical reaction {r.sum_reactions()[2] / 1e6:.3f} MN for {weight / 1e6:.3f} MN of deck; "
      f"cable off its profile by {deviation * 1e3:.2f} mm")

# 3. the same hangers at their unstressed length: their length defect goes into the permanent case
m1, data1, _ = bridge()
sm1 = m1.build_suspension(**data1, hanger_fit_case=1)
r1 = m1.cable_static(case=1)
defl = min(r.displacement(n)[2] for n in data["hanger_deck_nodes"])
defl1 = min(r1.displacement(n)[2] for n in data1["hanger_deck_nodes"])
print(f"hangers: placed stress-free, the deck goes down by {-defl * 1e3:.1f} mm; at their unstressed length "
      f"(from {min(sm1['hanger_l0']):.3f} to {max(sm1['hanger_l0']):.3f} m), by {-defl1 * 1e3:.1f} mm")

# 4. two-stage correction: the unstressed lengths bring the cable back onto its profile
m2, data2, _ = bridge()
u = m2.solve_uelm(1, **data2, tol=1e-10)
r2 = m2.cable_static(case=1)
deviation2 = max(abs(r2.displacement(n)[2]) for n in u["cable_nodes"][1:-1])
print(f"two stages: deviation {u['deviation_stage1'] * 1e3:.2f} mm before correction, {deviation2 * 1e3:.4f} mm after, "
      f"{u['iterations']} iterations; total unstressed length {sum(u['l0']):.3f} m")
assert deviation2 < 1e-7 and abs(r.sum_reactions()[2] - weight - 7850.0 * 0.30 * 9.80665 * shape["total_l0"]) < 1e-6 * weight

8.8 Tendons in shells#

Syntax
m.add_shell_tendon(points, P0, mu=0.0, k=0.0, jack='start', slab=0)  →  int

ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
pointsm—polyline of the tendon at its actual position (x, y, z), within the thickness of the shells
P0N—stressing force
mu, k1/rad, 1/m0friction in curves; line friction, loss e^{-k\,s} over the length travelled s (k is not multiplied by μ, unlike add_tendon)
jack—'start'live anchorage: 'start', 'end' or 'both'

The tendon is described independently of the mesh; add_shell_prestress places its equivalent loads (anchorages, deviations, friction) in a load case, like an ordinary loading.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_cable_coque.py
# Tendon in a shell slab: 10 m x 2 m strip, straight tendon
# 8 cm below the mid-surface, anchored at both edges; resulting camber.
import keta

Lx, Ly, t, E = 10.0, 2.0, 0.25, 30e9
nx, ny = 20, 4
m = keta.Model()
concrete = m.add_material(e=E, nu=0.0)
g = [[m.add_node(Lx * i / nx, Ly * j / ny, 0.0) for i in range(nx + 1)] for j in range(ny + 1)]
for j in range(ny):
    for i in range(nx):
        m.add_shell(g[j][i], g[j][i + 1], g[j + 1][i + 1], t, concrete, slab=1)
        m.add_shell(g[j][i], g[j + 1][i + 1], g[j + 1][i], t, concrete, slab=1)
for j in range(ny + 1):
    m.add_support(g[j][0], dz=True)
    m.add_support(g[j][nx], dz=True)
m.add_support(g[0][0], dx=True, dy=True, rz=True)
m.add_support(g[0][nx], dy=True)

P, e = 1.0e6, -0.08
tendon = m.add_shell_tendon([(0.0, Ly / 2, e), (Lx, Ly / 2, e)], P0=P, mu=0.0, k=0.0, jack="start")
m.add_shell_prestress(tendon, factor=1.0, case=1)
case_ = m.analyze().case(1)
w = sum(case_.displacement(g[j][nx // 2])[2] for j in range(ny + 1)) / (ny + 1)
I = Ly * t**3 / 12
print(f"mean camber at midspan: {w * 1e3:.3f} mm")
print(f"equivalent beam, M = P e = {P * -e / 1e3:.0f} kN.m: M L²/(8 E I) = {P * -e * Lx**2 / (8 * E * I) * 1e3:.3f} mm")
assert abs(case_.sum_reactions()[2]) < 1e-3          # self-equilibrated equivalent loads
print("resultant of the vertical reactions: zero (self-equilibrated equivalent loads)")

8.9 Exact profiles and tendon options#

Syntax
m.add_tendon(bars, P0, segments=[dict(s0, s1, cy=(c0, c1, c2, c3), cz=(…))], exact_angle=False,
             phi_abs=0.0, non_simul=1, relaxation='ec2', relax_teb_days=0.0, thermal_loss=0.0,
             free_runs=[], force_profile=[], …)  →  int
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
segmentsmNoneprofile made of contiguous cubic pieces, e(s) = c_0 + c_1\,\delta + c_2\,\delta^2 + c_3\,\delta^3, \delta = s - s_0; replaces profile
exact_angle—Falseaxial force P\cos\alpha instead of the small-slope approximation N = P
phi_abs1/m0linear friction independent of μ: P(s) = P_0\,e^{-\varphi s}
non_simul—1number of units stressed successively; each undergoes (n-1)/2n of the elastic loss it causes
relaxation—'ec2'relaxation law: 'ec2' (EN 1992-1-1 clause 3.3.2) or 'bpel' (BPEL 91, equivalent time)
relax_teb_days, thermal_lossdays, Pa0steam-cured pretensioning: equivalent time added to the relaxation, thermal loss at the release of the bed
free_runsm[]free lengths [(s0, s1)] of an external tendon: the unintentional deviation k does not act there
force_profilem, N[]force imposed point by point [(s, P)], piecewise linear, constant beyond the extreme points; replaces friction, unintentional deviation and anchorage draw-in

A profile described by cubic segments keeps its curvature: a parabola is no longer reduced to a series of chords, and the equivalent loads (radial thrust Pe′′) are those of the actual profile. Over a free length, an external tendon does not touch its duct: its force remains constant there as long as the profile is straight there, and resumes its decrease beyond. An imposed force describes a tendon whose tension is known otherwise (measurement, other analysis): the equivalent loads are then those of this force, tangential force P′ included. For pretensioning, force_profile describes the transmission law: the release of the bed (mode='release' of the tension key, Section 8.2) transfers this force to the concrete by bond.

8.10 Tendon in a static load case#

Syntax
m.add_tendon_loads(tendon, case=1, fraction=1.0)  →  int
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
tendon——identifier returned by add_tendon
case—1load case receiving the equivalent loads
fraction—1fraction of the force after friction and anchorage draw-in

Outside staged construction, the equivalent loads of a tendon are placed in an ordinary case, without time-dependent losses. The case is then combined with the others. In a staged construction analysis, this function is not used: the stages themselves stress the tendons.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_cable_statique.py
# Tendon in a static load case: exact parabolic profile, simply supported beam.
import keta

L, P, f = 20.0, 2e6, 0.5
m = keta.Model()
nd = [m.add_node(L * i / 8, 0, 0) for i in range(9)]
s = m.add_section_rect(b=0.6, h=1.2)
b = [m.add_beam(nd[i], nd[i + 1], s, m.concrete()) for i in range(8)]
m.add_support(nd[0], dx=True, dy=True, dz=True, rx=True, rz=True)
m.add_support(nd[-1], dy=True, dz=True, rz=True)
# e(s) = -4 f s (L - s) / L2: a single cubic segment (c0, c1, c2, c3)
tendon = m.add_tendon(b, P0=P, segments=[dict(s0=0.0, s1=L, cz=(0.0, -4 * f / L, 4 * f / L**2))],
                      mu=0.0, k=0.0, slip=0.0)
print("equivalent loads added:", m.add_tendon_loads(tendon, case=1))
c = m.analyze().case(1)
mid = c.bar_forces(b[3])[-1]
print(f"midspan: N = {mid[1] / 1e3:.1f} kN, My = {mid[5] / 1e3:.1f} kN.m (P f = {P * f / 1e3:.1f})")
print(f"vertical reaction at A = {abs(dict(c.reactions())[nd[0]][2]):.3f} N (statically determinate: 0)")
Output
equivalent loads added: 66
midspan: N = -2000.0 kN, My = 1000.0 kN.m (P f = 1000.0)
vertical reaction at A = 0.000 N (statically determinate: 0)
