Chapter 22 Section checking tools

These module functions apply to a section or to a slab point, from internal forces obtained from an analysis (or given). They do not depend on a model.

22.1 Reinforced concrete section under combined bending and axial force#

Syntax
keta.rc_design_section(rings, bars, fck, fcd, N=0.0, My=0.0, Mz=0.0, fyd=500e6/1.15,
                       ftd=525e6/1.15, eps_ud=0.025, Es=200e9, omega_max=1.0)  →  dict
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
ringsm—polygonal contours of the section in (y, z): outer contour counter-clockwise, voids clockwise
barsm—reinforcing bars (y, z, weight): the area of bar i is ω × weighti
fck, fcdPa—characteristic and design strengths of the concrete
N, My, MzN, N·m0design internal forces (N negative in compression, conventions of Chapter 3)
fyd, ftd, eps_ud, EsPa, Pa, —, Pa500e6/1.15; 525e6/1.15; 0.025; 200e9design yield strength and design tensile strength, ultimate strain and modulus of the reinforcement
omega_max—1upper bound of ω

Finds the smallest reinforcement coefficient ω such that the section resists the internal forces at ULS (EN 1992-1-1, parabola-rectangle diagram). Returns dict(feasible, As, omega, plane, resultants): feasibility, total reinforcement area [m²], coefficient, strain plane (ε₀, κy, κz) and resisting forces.

22.2 Rectangular or T section: service, resistance, shear#

Syntax
s = keta.RcSection(*, b, h, d, As, fck, bf=0, hf=0, d2=0, As2=0, fctm=0, Ecm=0, gamma_c=1.5,
                   alpha_cc=1, fyk=500e6, Es=200e9, gamma_s=1.15)
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
b, hm—width (of the web) and overall depth
dm—effective depth of the tension reinforcement As

fckPa—characteristic strength of the concrete
bf, hfm0width and thickness of the compression flange (T section)
d2, As2m, m²0position and area of the compression (top) reinforcement

Conventions specific to this object: the coordinate z is measured downwards from the top fibre; the axial force N is positive in tension and applied at the centroid of the gross section; the moment M is positive when it puts the bottom fibre in tension (span moment); stresses are positive in tension. # Reinforced concrete rectangular section 0.30 x 0.60 m, 4 HA20 (d = 0.55 m), C30/37:
# states I and II, cracking moment, crack width, curvature, deflection,
# resisting moment, interaction diagram, shear force and punching.
import math
import keta

As = 4 * math.pi * 0.020**2 / 4
s = keta.RcSection(b=0.30, h=0.60, d=0.55, As=As, fck=30e6)
M = 250e3                                            # service moment (positive in span)
for phi in (0.0, 2.0):
    u = s.uncracked(0.0, M, phi)
    c = s.cracked(0.0, M, phi)
    print(f"phi = {phi}: Mcr = {s.cracking_moment(0.0, phi) / 1e3:7.2f} kN.m; state I x = {u['x']:.4f} m, "
          f"I = {u['I'] * 1e4:.3f} dm4; state II x = {c['x']:.4f} m, sigma_c = {c['sigma_c_top'] / 1e6:6.2f} MPa, "
          f"sigma_s = {c['sigma_s'] / 1e6:6.1f} MPa")
w = s.crack_width(0.0, M, 0.0, cover=0.04, bar_dia=0.020)
print(f"crack width: wk = {w['wk'] * 1e3:.3f} mm (sr,max = {w['s_r_max'] * 1e3:.1f} mm, "
      f"hc,ef = {w['hc_ef'] * 1e3:.1f} mm)")
k = s.curvature(0.0, M, 0.0)
print(f"curvature: kappa_I = {k['kappa_I'] * 1e3:.4f}, kappa_II = {k['kappa_II'] * 1e3:.4f}, "
      f"zeta = {k['zeta']:.4f}, kappa = {k['kappa'] * 1e3:.4f} 1/km")
L, q = 8.0, 30e3
f = s.deflection_ss(L, lambda x: q * x * (L - x) / 2, phi=2.0)
print(f"long-term deflection of a span of {L} m under {q / 1e3:.0f} kN/m: {f * 1e3:.2f} mm (L/{L / f:.0f})")
r = s.mrd(0.0)
print(f"MRd = {r['M_Rd'] / 1e3:.2f} kN.m, x = {r['x'] * 1e3:.1f} mm, steel yielding: {r['steel_yields']}")
curve = s.interaction(9)
print("interaction (N kN, MRd kN.m):", [(round(n / 1e3), round(mm / 1e3, 1)) for n, mm in curve])

VRd = keta.vrd_c(bw=0.30, d=0.55, Asl=As, fck=30e6)
print(f"VRd,c = {VRd / 1e3:.2f} kN; shear reinforcement for 300 kN (cot = 2): "
      f"{keta.asw_s(300e3, 0.9 * 0.55, 500e6 / 1.15, 2.0) * 1e4:.3f} cm2/m; "
      f"minimum {keta.asw_min_s(30e6, 500e6, 0.30) * 1e4:.3f} cm2/m")
nu = keta.nu_shear(30e6)
print(f"VRd,max (cot = 2) = {keta.vrd_max(0.30, 0.9 * 0.55, nu, 20e6, 2.0) / 1e3:.1f} kN")
p = keta.punching(VEd=600e3, a=0.4, b=0.4, d=0.25, rho_ly=0.01, rho_lz=0.01, fck=30e6, beta=1.15)
print(f"punching: u1 = {p['u1']:.3f} m, vEd = {p['vEd'] / 1e6:.3f} MPa, vRd,c = {p['vRd_c'] / 1e6:.3f} MPa")

The tendons are added to the polygonal section of the design. A bonded tendon follows the strain plane from its initial strain; its bound ε_ud acts at pivot A through the additional strain ε_ud − Δε_p(0). An unbonded tendon keeps its initial stress increased by Δσ_p,ULS (capped by the law), whatever the plane (dsigma_uls=0: fixed force). The resisting moment also returns the plane (ε₀, κy, κz), the domain parameter τ (pivot A between 0 and 1, B between 1 and 2, C between 2 and 3) and the stress of each tendon. The diagram sweeps the ultimate planes from pivot A to crushing. The isostatic action is Σ(−P, −P z, +P y) with P = A_p σ(Δε_p(0)); this is the effect of the tendon on a simply supported beam. Subtracted from a total effect, it leaves the hyperstatic part. tendon_initial_strain adds to the strain of σ_pm the shortening −σ_cp / E_cm of the concrete at the level of the tendon.

prestressed_m_kappa integrates a rectangular fibre section at constant axial force; an unbonded tendon keeps its initial force there (without stiffness). At zero curvature, the moment reported is the one that keeps the section straight under the prestressing. The parabola-rectangle law sets the stress to zero beyond ε_cu2. Up to and including the ultimate curvature, the equilibrium returned keeps each fibre and each bonded tendon within the bound of its law; beyond it, it is the equilibrium after crushing of the fibres that have exceeded it. prestressed_ultimate_point returns this ultimate point, that is, the largest curvature of direction α (κy = κ cos α, κz = κ sin α; α = π for a bottom fibre in tension) whose equilibrium respects the strain bounds of the laws. A moment-curvature curve stops at this point, at the first limit strain reached. The concrete bound is checked at the centre of the fibres: the ultimate curvature exceeds that of the section edge by at most half a layer. A bar of rebars may carry an initial strain eps0, the one it has when the plane is zero at its level (for example the compression that creep and shrinkage have left in it). Its law is then read at eps0 + ε; its strain bound and eps_steel_max apply to this sum.

Three functions read the same fibre rectangle without going through a curve: fiber_resultants returns the resultants (N, My, Mz) of a strain plane (ε₀, κy, κz), with ε(y, z) = ε₀ + κy z − κz y; fiber_state returns the plane in equilibrium with an action (N, My, Mz), each fibre within the bounds of its law, and refuses an action beyond the capacity of the section; fiber_interaction returns the My-Mz interaction curve at constant N, the ultimate point of n_alpha bending directions distributed around the full turn (α = 2π i / n_alpha).

Without concrete, the concrete follows the parabola-rectangle of Table 3.1 of EN 1992-1-1 for fck, at fcd. With concrete, it follows the law given, which also sets the pivots: crushing at its limit compressive strain, pivot C at the strain where it reaches its greatest compression. Two laws are accepted: the parabola-rectangle with free exponent and strains, and the law of Section 3.1.5 (kind='sargin'), to be read with design values. With exponent 1, the first is the bilinear relation of Section 3.1.7(2), which bilinear_concrete(fck, fcd) builds with ε_c3 and ε_cu3 of Table 3.1. A law that carries tension is refused. With deduct_displaced, the concrete displaced by the steels is deducted: the disc of displaced_area of each tendon and a disc of the area of each bar. Without this option, no concrete is deducted at the location of the steels.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_section_precontrainte.py
# Rectangular beam 0.40 x 0.80 m in C40/50, one tendon of 1500 mm² (Y1860S7) 0.30 m below
# the centroid, stressed to 1000 MPa: resisting moment with a bonded, then an unbonded
# tendon (compared with the parabola-rectangle block by hand), N-M diagram, isostatic action
# and moment-curvature relation up to the ultimate point.
import math
import keta

b, h, fck = 0.40, 0.80, 40e6
fcd = 0.85 * fck / 1.5
rings = [[(-b / 2, -h / 2), (b / 2, -h / 2), (b / 2, h / 2), (-b / 2, h / 2)]]
Ap, zp, sigma_pm = 1500e-6, -0.30, 1000e6
eps_p0 = keta.tendon_initial_strain(sigma_pm)          # 1000 MPa / Ep (the concrete is not yet compressed)
tendon = dict(y=0.0, z=zp, Ap=Ap, eps_p0=eps_p0)

r = keta.prestressed_section_resistance(rings, fck, fcd, [tendon], theta=math.pi)  # positive bending: My < 0
print(f"bonded: MRd = {r['M_R'] / 1e3:.1f} kN.m, sigma_p = {r['tendon_stress'][0] / 1e6:.1f} MPa, "
      f"pivot {'ABC'[min(int(r['tau']), 2)]}")
# by hand: the tendon reaches the inclined branch; block 17/21 b x fcd at 99/238 x from the compressed edge
P = Ap * r["tendon_stress"][0]
x = P / (17 / 21 * b * fcd)
print(f"  block by hand: x = {x:.4f} m, MRd = {P * (h / 2 - zp - 99 / 238 * x) / 1e3:.1f} kN.m")

unbonded = dict(tendon, bonded=False)                  # lump-sum stress increase of 100 MPa
r = keta.prestressed_section_resistance(rings, fck, fcd, [unbonded], theta=math.pi)
P = Ap * (sigma_pm + 100e6)
x = P / (17 / 21 * b * fcd)
print(f"unbonded: MRd = {r['M_R'] / 1e3:.1f} kN.m (by hand {P * (h / 2 - zp - 99 / 238 * x) / 1e3:.1f})")

d = keta.prestressed_section_diagram(rings, fck, fcd, [tendon], math.pi / 2, n_points=7)
print("diagram (N kN, My kN.m):", [(round(n / 1e3), round(my / 1e3)) for n, my, mz in d])

o = keta.prestress_decomposition([tendon], total=(-1.5e6, 0.30e6, 0.0))
print(f"isostatic action (N, My) = ({o['isostatic'][0] / 1e3:.0f} kN, {o['isostatic'][1] / 1e3:.0f} kN.m), "
      f"hyperstatic part My = {o['hyperstatic'][1] / 1e3:.0f} kN.m")

concrete = dict(kind="concrete_pr", fcd=fcd)
fibres, tendons = (b, h, 1, 200), [dict(tendon, A=Ap)]
ultimate = keta.prestressed_ultimate_point(fibres, concrete, tendons, alpha=math.pi)   # bottom fibre in tension
print(f"ultimate point: kappa_u = {-ultimate['kappa_y'] * 1e3:.2f} 1/km, M = {-ultimate['My'] / 1e3:.1f} kN.m")
mk = keta.prestressed_m_kappa(fibres, concrete, tendons, [ultimate["kappa_y"] * i / 5 for i in range(6)])
print("M-kappa (1/km, kN.m):", [(round(-k * 1e3, 1), round(-m / 1e3)) for k, m, e0 in mk])

Three service states share the same section description and the same result. prestress_service_state is the uncracked state: elastic concrete of modulus E_cm, tension included. cracked_service_state is the cracked state: the concrete carries E_cm ε where it is in compression and nothing where it is in tension. nonlinear_service_state applies its own law to each material. In all three, a bonded tendon carries the strain Δε_p(0) + ε_c, ε_c the concrete strain at its level, an unbonded tendon keeps that of Δε_p(0), and a reinforcing bar carries ε_c, increased where applicable by its initial strain eps0 (the strain that creep and shrinkage have left in it). Without a tendon, these are the states of a reinforced concrete section.

The result gives, tension positive: tendon_stress, the stress of each tendon; concrete_stress, that of the concrete at its level; bar_stress, that of each bar; point_stress, that of the concrete at each point of points, in order; plane, the strain plane (e0, ky, kz), ε = e0 + ky z − kz y; decompressed, true if a fibre of the contours is in tension. A point outside the concrete (beyond the contours, in a void or in the disc displaced by a tendon) is refused. fibre_max and fibre_min are the extreme fibres, dict(y, z, stress): in the uncracked state, the contour vertex in greatest tension and the one in greatest compression (along an edge at the extreme stress, one of its ends); in the other two, the vertices of greatest and of smallest strain, with the concrete stress at that point (zero at the vertex in greatest tension of a cracked section). These values are to be compared with the stress limits; keta does not return a verdict.

In the uncracked state, the concrete stress is a plane over the section: with bonded tendons, N / A + M z / I of the transformed section A_c + (E_p / E_cm) A_p + (E_s / E_cm) A_s under the action and the decompression forces A_p E_p Δε_p(0) of the tendons, the bars being stress-free in this state; with unbonded tendons, that of the concrete section and its bars under the action and the tendon forces.

The cracked state seeks the strain plane whose resultants are the action. It is unique when the section can carry it. Otherwise (tension with no bonded steel to resist it, moment on concrete alone), the call is refused. For a section that the action leaves wholly in compression, the result is that of the uncracked state. EN 1992-1-1 clause 7.1(2) retains the uncracked state as long as the concrete tension, fibre_max of prestress_service_state, does not exceed f_ct,eff: service_state, described below, makes this choice. For a long-term action, give the effective modulus E_cm / (1 + φ).

The nonlinear state uses the laws as they are given; for a service state, they are defined with characteristic or mean strengths, without partial factor. Without strand, the tendons follow the Y1860S7 strand at its characteristic strengths (γ_s = 1); the bars follow the bilinear curve E_s, fy, then ft at eps_ud. A state in which the concrete, a bonded tendon or a bar exceeds the strain bound of its law is refused, as it is no longer a service state. With a softening law (descending branch of Expression (3.14) beyond ε_c1, tension softening), the plane returned is an equilibrium that is not necessarily unique.

Important

Without displaced_area, a tendon removes nothing from the concrete of the contours. With it, it removes a disc of that area, whose centre is offset from the tendon by (displaced_dy, displaced_dz): the area of the duct as long as it is not grouted (net section), the tendon's own area once grouted or for a pre-tensioned strand, that is A_c + (E_p / E_cm − 1) A_p. Without this deduction, the relative error on the concrete stress is of the order of A_p (1 / A_c + e² / I_c), e the eccentricity of the tendon. The disc must remain within the concrete of the contours and outside the other discs. The section losses read the same concrete; the resisting moments deduct it only on request (deduct_displaced).

These functions return values (limits, ratios, moments, areas, widths) and do not return a verdict. stress_limits gives the five stress limits of clause 7.2 of EN 1992-1-1: concrete_characteristic = k1 f_ck (concrete under the characteristic combination, classes XD, XF and XS), concrete_quasi_permanent = k2 f_ck (threshold of linear creep under quasi-permanent loads), bar = k3 f_yk and bar_imposed = k4 f_yk (reinforcement under the characteristic combination, and under imposed deformation), tendon = k5 f_pk (prestressing steel). stress_ratios divides the stresses of a state by these limits: concrete_compression is the compression of the vertex in greatest compression, bar_tension and tendon_tension the greatest tensions of the bars and of the tendons; the ratios bear the name of their limit. The combination under which the state was computed decides which ratios are to be read.

service_state applies Section 7.1(2): it computes the uncracked state, reads its greatest concrete stress flexural_tension, and returns this state as long as it does not exceed fct_eff, the cracked state beyond (cracked). The choice is made on the given action alone: a section already cracked by an earlier, larger action is computed directly by cracked_service_state.

section_cracking_moment returns the moment M_cr of direction θ which, added to sustained, brings the first fibre of the uncracked section to fct: M_cr = (fct − σ_cp) W, where σ_cp (fibre["stress"]) is the stress of this fibre under the prestressing and sustained, and W (modulus) the section modulus of the transformed section in this direction. If a fibre already exceeds fct under sustained alone, the call is refused. With prestress=False, this is the moment M_rep of EN 1992-2 clause 6.1(109), computed without the prestressing; brittle_failure_steel derives from it the minimum steel against brittle failure A_s,min = M_rep / (z f_yk) (Expression 6.101a), reduced by the share A_p Δσ_p of the tendons counted, Δσ_p = min(0.4 f_ptk; 500 MPa) (Expression 6.101b). ultimate_lever_arm gives the lever arm z: on the ultimate plane that carries N with a moment of direction θ, the distance between the resultant of the compressed concrete and that of the bars in tension. The resisting moment to compare with M_cr is read with prestressed_section_resistance.

section_crack_width computes the crack width of Section 7.3.4 from the cracked state: w_k = s_r,max (ε_sm − ε_cm). Along the strain gradient, h is the depth of the section, x the depth of the compression zone and d the depth of the centroid of the bars in tension; the effective area A_c,eff is the concrete located less than h_c,ef = min(2.5 (h − d); (h − x) / 3) from the fibre in greatest tension, cut from the contours, voids deducted. The bars and the bonded tendons whose centre lies in this band enter ρ_p,eff = (A_s + ξ1 A_p') / A_c,eff, with ξ1 = √(ξ φ_s / φ_p), or √ξ if there are tendons only. σ_s is the greatest tension of the bars; without a bar in tension, the stress change of the bonded tendon from zero concrete strain at its level. The result gives h, d, x, hc_ef, Ac_eff, As, Ap, xi1, rho_p_eff, sigma_s, d_eps (ε_sm − ε_cm, with its floor 0.6 σ_s / E_s), s_r_max, wide_spacing (Expression 7.14 used) and wk. A section that the action leaves with no compression zone (a tie) is refused; crack_width_chain then applies the same expressions to the effective area and to the steel stress that are given.

Important

Expression (7.10) of EN 1992-1-1 carries ξ1 since the 2010 corrigendum (AC:2010); earlier texts and guides write ξ1², and the German version, like most design software, keeps this form. xi1_squared=True reproduces it; the choice depends on the text applied.

section_losses takes up the time-dependent losses of section_prestress_losses, counting the reinforcing bars: each steel, tendon or bar, follows the concrete at its level. The bars restrain creep and shrinkage, go into compression and unload the concrete; the loss of the tendon is reduced, but the concrete loses more compression. tendons and bars are lists of loss dicts (for a bar: stress, its stress at t₀, and the tension it loses per cause); concrete_change = (s0, sz, sy) is the change of concrete stress s0 + sz z + sy y between t₀ and t, positive where the compression decreases. eps_p0_after remains the initial strain of the tendon after losses, and that of each bar is its initial strain after losses. The section rebuilt with these values (eps_p0 of each tendon, fourth value (y, z, weight, eps0) of each bar) is, under the same action, in the state after losses; a subsequent action (live load, cracking, resisting moment) is computed from that state. section_loss_history takes the same bars (bars, omega, Es) and counts them in the losses at each age.

22.6 Composite section: several materials without a mesh#

Syntax
keta.composite_section_resultants(parts, plane, steels=[])  →  (N, My, Mz)
keta.composite_section_state(parts, action, steels=[])  →  dict(plane, part_resultants, steel_stress)
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
parts——parts: dict(rings, law), each material on its law (dict) and on its contours (outer counter-clockwise, voids clockwise)
steels—[]point steels: dict(y, z, A, law, eps0=0), eps0 the initial strain of the steel when the plane is zero
plane—, 1/m—strain plane (e0, ky, kz), ε = e0 + ky z − kz y
actionN, N·m—action (N, My, Mz) at the origin of the axes

A composite section (steel girder under a concrete slab, concrete cast in two strengths, encased section) is described by its parts, each with its law and its contours; all share the same strain plane, as do the point steels. The resultants of each part are integrals over its contours (Green's formula), without a mesh. The contours of two parts do not overlap: a part lying inside another is drawn as a void of the latter. Laws of a part: 'concrete_pr', 'sargin', 'concrete_tension_softening', 'elastic', 'steel' (bilinear with plateau) and 'steel_hardening'; of a point steel: 'elastic', 'steel', 'steel_hardening', 'strand'.

composite_section_state seeks the plane whose resultants are the action; it returns this plane, what each part carries (part_resultants) and the stress of each point steel. A state that takes a part or a steel beyond the limit strain of its law (crushing, rupture) is refused, as it is a matter for a resistance calculation. With a softening law, the plane returned is an equilibrium that is not necessarily unique.
