Chapter 13 Staged construction

13.1 Principle#

The staged construction analysis follows the construction of a structure: the structure evolves (bars cast or erected, temporary supports, closures, stressing operations, dismantling) and the loads apply to the structure present at the time they act. Between two dates, the concrete creeps and shrinks and the tendons lose part of their force.

keta performs this analysis by incremental superposition. The stages are run through in the order of their dates; each event (activation, loading, stressing, support removal, creep increment) is computed linearly on the substructure active at that instant, and its effect is added to the state reached. The forces “locked” into the structure by the construction history are thus preserved. In particular:

  • an activated bar is born stress-free in the current deformed geometry;
  • a deactivated bar or a removed support returns to the remaining structure the forces it carried;
  • the loads of a case applied in a stage remain applied in the following stages;
  • between two dates, each concrete bar with a time-dependent law (Chapter 7) receives the creep strain increment of its stress history (Boltzmann superposition) and the shrinkage increment; the tendons undergo the coupled time-dependent losses (shrinkage, creep, relaxation).

13.2 Staged construction analysis#

Syntax
m.phased(phases, creep_engine='exact', stations=11, exact_losses=True, modulus=None,
         creep_substeps_per_decade=4, creep_along_bar=False, origin_ledgers=False,
         keep_structures=False)  →  Phased
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
phases——list of stage dictionaries, in chronological order (Section 13.3)
creep_engine—'exact'creep calculation method: 'exact' (Boltzmann superposition with the law itself), 'kelvin' (Kelvin chains fitted to the law, one per loading event), 'aaem' (one step per interval: Δε = φ(t₂, t₁)·σ/E, without ageing coefficient; splitting an interval overstates the total creep), 'incremental' (incremental method by fibres)
modulus—Nonefunction f(bar, age in days) → instantaneous modulus [Pa] of each increment, at the age of the concrete of the bar at the date of the stage (bars with a concrete law); None: modulus of the material
stations—11output sections per bar
exact_losses—Truelosses of bonded tendons computed through the structure (otherwise section formula of EN 1992-1-1 clause 5.10.6 per interval)
creep_substeps_per_decade—4time substeps per decade of elapsed time within a creep interval
creep_along_bar—Falsecreep computed at each output section (otherwise one mean stress per bar, taken at mid-length)
origin_ledgers—Falsestorage of the cumulative state broken down by origin (loads, prestressing, losses)
keep_structures—Falsestorage of the active substructure of each stage, read by PhaseResult.tangent() (Section 16.7)
Important

With the default options, the creep of a bar is computed from the stress at mid-length and applied as a uniform strain: the approximation is valid only for bars that are short compared with the variation of the forces. For an accurate redistribution (closure, continuity), creep_along_bar=True is activated and creep_substeps_per_decade is increased (example of Section 13.6).

13.3 Description of the stages#

Each stage is a dictionary whose keys are the following; only the useful keys are given. An unknown key raises ValueError.

Table 13.1 — Keys of a stage
KeyValueEffect
nametextname of the stage (default “phase i”)
timedaysdate of the stage; creep acts between the dates
activate[bars]activated bars (cast or erected stress-free); bars never cited are active from the start
activate_age{bar: age}concrete age [days] of the activated bars (default 0: concrete cast at the date of the stage)
deactivate[bars]bars removed, forces returned
add_supports[dict(node, dx, dy, dz, rx, ry, rz)]supports added (by default translations restrained, rotations free)
remove_supports[nodes]supports removed, reactions returned
cases[cases]load cases applied from this stage onwards
tension[dict(tendon, fraction, mode, unbonded, group)]stressing and destressing of tendons (Section 8.2)
inject[tendons]grouting of the stressed ungrouted tendons
inject_partial[(tendon, s0, s1)]partial grouting between two abscissae
place_hinges, close_hinges[dict(bar, end=1, dofs='rz')]placing of a temporary hinge at one end of a bar (end 0: origin, 1: end; dofs: local components released, for example 'ry'); closure of the hinge
modify_sections[(bar, section)]change of section of an active bar (without time-dependent material)
regime'linear' or 'second_order'regime of the increments of the stage; at second order, equilibrium accounts for the total axial force
imperfection[(node, (6 values))]injected geometric imperfection (acts at second order)
cable_actions[dict(cable, kind, value)]actions on catenary cables: 'length' (unstrained length), 'horizontal' (target horizontal tension), 'end_tension', 'thermal' (relative elongation α·ΔT), 'rupture' (dynamic factor)
Note

A hinge placed by place_hinges is admitted only where the corresponding force is zero: it is placed before the bar is loaded. The closure close_hinges locks the relative rotation acquired, without force.

13.4 Results#

The Phased object contains one state per stage:

Syntax
len(r)  →  int
r.names()  →  [names of the stages]
r.phase(i)  →  PhaseResult

The state of the stage of index i (0 for the first) has the following attributes and methods:

ItemResult
name, timename and date of the stage
displacement(node)cumulative displacement at the end of the stage
increment_displacement(node)displacement increment of the stage
bar_forces(bar)cumulative forces at the output sections (x/L, N, Vy, Vz, T, My, Mz)
increment_bar_forces(bar)force increment of the stage
reactions()cumulative reactions
tendons()tendons: (id, stressing force, cumulative losses, effective force)
cables()catenary cables: (bar, active, L0, H, T1, T2)
Note

A removed support no longer carries a reaction: from the stage of its removal, it appears in reactions() with a zero reaction, its previous reaction having been returned to the structure.

13.5 Example: beam cast on a prop#

A 12 m beam is cast on a central prop, then the prop is removed. The material has no time-dependent law: the staged construction analysis is purely elastic.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_phasage_etai.py
# Staged construction without creep: beam of 2 x 6 m cast on a central prop, then the prop is removed;
# activations, addition and removal of a support, accumulation of the states.
import keta

L, q = 6.0, -25e3
m = keta.Model()
mat = m.add_material(e=30e9, nu=0.2, rho=0.0)        # no time-dependent law: elastic staging
sec = m.add_section_props(sx=0.3, iy=6.4e-3, iz=2e-3, ix=2e-3)
n = [m.add_node(L * i / 2, 0, 0) for i in range(5)]
b = [m.add_beam(n[i], n[i + 1], sec, mat) for i in range(4)]
m.add_support(n[0], dx=True, dy=True, dz=True, rx=True)
m.add_support(n[4], dy=True, dz=True)
for bi in b:
    m.add_udl(bi, wz=q, case=1)                      # weight of the fresh concrete

r = m.phased([
    dict(name="propping", time=0, activate=b, add_supports=[dict(node=n[2], dx=False, dy=True, dz=True)]),
    dict(name="casting", time=1, cases=[1]),
    dict(name="prop removal", time=28, remove_supports=[n[2]]),
])
for i in range(len(r)):
    p = r.phase(i)
    M = p.bar_forces(b[1])[-1][5]                    # section at mid-length
    R = dict(p.reactions())[n[0]][2]                 # end support
    dM = p.increment_bar_forces(b[1])[-1][5]
    print(f"{p.name:13}: M(mid) = {M / 1e3:8.2f} kN.m (increment {dM / 1e3:8.2f}), "
          f"end reaction = {R / 1e3:7.2f} kN, deflection = {p.displacement(n[2])[2] * 1e3:7.3f} mm")
print(f"on the prop (2 spans of 6 m): M = -q L²/8 = {-q * L * L / 8 / 1e3:.2f} kN.m, R = 3 q L/8 = {-3 * q * L / 8 / 1e3:.2f} kN")
print(f"after prop removal (12 m):   M = q (2L)²/8 = {q * (2 * L)**2 / 8 / 1e3:.2f} kN.m, R = q L = {-q * L / 1e3:.2f} kN")
Output
propping     : M(mid) =     0.00 kN.m (increment     0.00), end reaction =    0.00 kN, deflection =   0.000 mm
casting      : M(mid) =   112.50 kN.m (increment   112.50), end reaction =   56.25 kN, deflection =   0.000 mm
prop removal : M(mid) =  -450.00 kN.m (increment  -562.50), end reaction =  150.00 kN, deflection = -35.156 mm
on the prop (2 spans of 6 m): M = -q L²/8 = 112.50 kN.m, R = 3 q L/8 = 56.25 kN
after prop removal (12 m):   M = q (2L)²/8 = -450.00 kN.m, R = q L = 150.00 kN

At casting, the beam behaves as a two-span continuous beam; at the removal of the prop, the reaction of the prop (187.5 kN) is returned to the 12 m beam, whose final state is that of a statically determinate beam cast in one piece.

13.6 Example: closure of two spans#

Two precast spans of 20 m are placed on their supports at 20 days, with a hinge over the central support; they receive their permanent load at 28 days and are then made continuous by a closure at 60 days. Creep (Courbon non-ageing law, φ∞ = 2) gives rise over the support to a continuity moment, whose limit value is known: M∞ = Mc (φ∞ − φ(t₁ − t₀)) / (1 + φ∞), Mc being the moment of the beam cast continuous.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_phasage_clavage.py
# Staged construction: two precast spans of 20 m placed as simply supported, then made continuous
# by a closure over the central support; creep redistribution of the support moment.
import math
import keta

L, q, E = 20.0, -50e3, 35e9
m = keta.Model()
concrete = m.add_material(e=E, nu=0.2, rho=0.0)
m.set_concrete_law(concrete, "COURBON", e_direct=E)   # non-ageing creep, phi(inf) = 2
sec = m.add_section_props(sx=0.8, iy=0.25, iz=0.1, ix=0.1)   # rigid in shear
n = [m.add_node(L * i / 4, 0, 0) for i in range(9)]
b = [m.add_beam(n[i], n[i + 1], sec, concrete) for i in range(8)]
m.add_support(n[0], dx=True, dy=True, dz=True, rx=True)
m.add_support(n[4], dy=True, dz=True)
m.add_support(n[8], dy=True, dz=True)
for bi in b:
    m.add_udl(bi, wz=q, case=1)                    # self-weight + superimposed dead loads

phases = [
    dict(name="placing", time=20, place_hinges=[dict(bar=b[3], end=1, dofs="ry")]),
    dict(name="loading", time=28, cases=[1]),
    dict(name="closure", time=60, close_hinges=[dict(bar=b[3], end=1, dofs="ry")]),
    dict(name="1 year", time=393),
    dict(name="30 years", time=28 + 30 * 365),
]
r = m.phased(phases, creep_along_bar=True, creep_substeps_per_decade=32)
print("stages:", r.names())
for i in range(len(r)):
    p = r.phase(i)
    Ma = p.bar_forces(b[3])[-1][5] + 0.0         # moment at the end of the bar over the support
    print(f"{p.name:11} t = {p.time:6.0f} days: M(support) = {abs(Ma) / 1e3:8.2f} kN.m, "
          f"midspan deflection = {p.displacement(n[2])[2] * 1e3:8.3f} mm")
r0 = m.phased(phases)                            # default options: creep at mid-bar
print(f"default options, 30 years: M(support) = {r0.phase(4).bar_forces(b[3])[-1][5] / 1e3:.2f} kN.m")

# Check: non-ageing law, exact limit M = Mc (phi(inf) - phi(t1 - t0)) / (1 + phi(inf))
phi = lambda d: 2 * (1 - math.exp(-0.006 * d))
Mc = -q * L * L / 8                              # moment of the beam cast continuous
M30 = Mc * (phi(30 * 365) - phi(60 - 28)) / (1 + phi(30 * 365))
print(f"beam cast continuous: Mc = {Mc / 1e3:.2f} kN.m; limit by creep: {M30 / 1e3:.2f} kN.m")
Output
stages: ['placing', 'loading', 'closure', '1 year', '30 years']
placing     t =     20 days: M(support) =     0.00 kN.m, midspan deflection =    0.000 mm
loading     t =     28 days: M(support) =     0.00 kN.m, midspan deflection =  -11.905 mm
closure     t =     60 days: M(support) =     0.00 kN.m, midspan deflection =  -16.062 mm
1 year      t =    393 days: M(support) =  1372.57 kN.m, midspan deflection =  -22.854 mm
30 years    t =  10978 days: M(support) =  1374.65 kN.m, midspan deflection =  -23.920 mm
default options, 30 years: M(support) = 1437.01 kN.m
beam cast continuous: Mc = 2500.00 kN.m; limit by creep: 1375.51 kN.m

With creep computed at each output section and fine substeps, the moment at 30 years (1374.65 kN·m) reaches the theoretical limit value (1375.51 kN·m) to within 0.06 %; the default options give here 4 % too much.

13.8 Incremental launching#

Syntax
m.launch_on_path(chain, piers, advances, kind="straight", origin=(0, 0, 0), heading=0.0, radius=0.0,
                 grade=0.0, stations=5, path_tol=1e-6)  →  dict(positions, m_min, m_max)
keta.launch_path_point(s, kind="straight", origin=(0, 0, 0), heading=0.0, radius=0.0, grade=0.0)  →  (point, tangent)
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
chain——deck bars in the order of launching, from the rear to the front; their nodes are on the path
piersm—ground abscissae of the piers, along the path
advancesm—positions computed: ground abscissa of the front end of the deck
kind—"straight"path: "straight" (straight line), "circular" (circle in plan), "helical" (circle in plan on a gradient)
origin, headingm, rad(0, 0, 0), 0point of abscissa 0 of the path and plan angle of its tangent, from +X towards +Y
radiusm0radius in plan (positive: left-hand curve)
grade—0gradient: rise per metre of plan length, dz/dsplan (straight and helical paths; refused for a circular path)
stations—5output sections per bar for the moment envelopes
path_tolm1e-6allowed deviation between a deck node and the path

A deck can be launched along its axis only if this axis is a curve of constant curvature and torsion: straight line, circle in plan or helix. The mesh does not move, the supports move. At each position, a pier bears on the deck at its contact abscissa, even between two nodes, and holds it vertically and laterally in the axes of the path; the first pier engaged also holds the longitudinal direction and the torsion. When the contact falls between two nodes, the bar is split at the contact for the analysis of this position; a contact less than one ten-millionth of a bar from a node bears on the node. A split bar must be a prismatic bar on its line of nodes, away from any cable: otherwise the analysis is refused, and a node placed at the contact removes the refusal. The deck is loaded by its self-weight; the supports, cases and combinations of the model are ignored. Each position returns per pier the contact abscissa along the deck from its rear end (contact_s, NaN outside the deck), the vertical reaction, the lateral guide force and the moment My of the deck at the contact (moment), as well as the piers in uplift (negative reaction) and the front and rear overhangs. A position with fewer than two piers under the deck is flagged unstable and is not computed. Two piers at the same contact share one support, carried by the first one in the list. m_min and m_max are the envelopes of the moment My at the output sections of the deck bars over the stable positions.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_lancement.py
# Steel deck of 60 m (0.45 m² of steel) launched on a circular alignment of 300 m radius,
# piers every 30 m. Reactions and overhang at a few positions, envelope of the moments.
import keta

R, L, n = 300.0, 60.0, 60
m = keta.Model()
steel = m.add_material(210e9, 0.3, rho=7850.0)
section = m.add_section_props(0.45, 0.60, 2.5, 0.9)          # area, Iy, Iz, J
nodes = [m.add_node(*keta.launch_path_point(L * i / n, kind="circular", radius=R)[0]) for i in range(n + 1)]
deck = [m.add_beam(a, b, section, steel) for a, b in zip(nodes, nodes[1:])]

piers = [0.0, 30.0, 60.0]
r = m.launch_on_path(deck, piers, [20.0, 45.0, 58.0, 75.0], kind="circular", radius=R)
for p in r["positions"]:
    if not p["stable"]:
        print(f"advance {p['advance']:5.1f} m: fewer than two piers under the deck")
        continue
    reactions = ", ".join(f"{v / 1e3:7.1f}" for v in p["reaction"])
    print(f"advance {p['advance']:5.1f} m: reactions [{reactions}] kN, front overhang "
          f"{p['front_overhang']:.1f} m" + (" (uplift)" if p["uplift"] else ""))
weight = 7850.0 * 0.45 * 9.80665 * L
print(f"deck weight {weight / 1e3:.1f} kN; extreme moments {min(r['m_min']) / 1e3:.0f} and "
      f"{max(r['m_max']) / 1e3:.0f} kN.m")
Output
advance  20.0 m: fewer than two piers under the deck
advance  45.0 m: reactions [ 1039.7,  1038.8,     0.0] kN, front overhang 15.0 m
advance  58.0 m: reactions [  141.4,  1937.1,     0.0] kN, front overhang 28.0 m
advance  75.0 m: reactions [    0.0,  1039.7,  1038.8] kN, front overhang 15.0 m
deck weight 2078.5 kN; extreme moments -1 and 13570 kN.m

13.9 Nonlinear staged construction#

Two analyses complement linear staged construction:

Syntax
m.staged_large_displacements(stages)  →  [{node: (dx, dy, dz, rx, ry, rz)}, …]
m.fiber_frame(sections, case=None, load_steps=10, corotational=False, control=None, stages=[])  →  dict

staged_large_displacements chains stages in large displacements: stages is a list of dict(activate=[bars], case=case, cable_l0={cable: L0}); the new nodes are placed stress-free on the deformed geometry (a segment erected in line with the deflected cantilever). The result gives, for each stage, the displacements of all the nodes.

fiber_frame analyses a bar frame with fibres (material nonlinearity, discretised rectangular section); its stages dict(activate, case, load_steps, duration, time_steps, activation_age) make the fibres creep between the stages according to a Kelvin chain given in the law of each section (creep=dict(a=[…], tau=[…], fck)), with imposed shrinkage (shrink=[(age, ε)]) and possible drying. The fibre laws are described in Chapter 17.

Exampleexamples/manual/ex_phasage_non_lineaire.py
# Special staged analyses: construction in large displacements (segment placed on the
# tangent of the deformed cantilever) and fibre column creeping between two stages.
import math
import keta

# 1) Cantilever of 4 m built in two segments of 2 m; a moment at the end of the first segment
m = keta.Model()
mat = m.add_material(e=2e11, nu=0.3)
sec = m.add_section_props(sx=1e-2, iy=1e-5, iz=1e-5, ix=2e-5)
nd = [m.add_node(0.5 * i, 0, 0) for i in range(9)]
b = [m.add_beam(nd[i], nd[i + 1], sec, mat) for i in range(8)]
m.fix(nd[0])
for x in nd[1:]:
    m.add_support(x, dy=True, rx=True, rz=True)
m.add_point_load(nd[4], my=0.2 * 2e6 / 2.0, case=1)        # end rotation of 0.2 rad
states = m.staged_large_displacements([dict(activate=b[:4], case=1), dict(activate=b[4:])])
print(f"stage 1: rotation at x = 2 m: {states[0][nd[4]][4]:.6f} rad, deflection {states[0][nd[4]][2] * 1e3:.3f} mm")
print(f"stage 2: rotation at x = 4 m: {states[1][nd[8]][4]:.6f} rad, deflection {states[1][nd[8]][2] * 1e3:.3f} mm")

# 2) Fibre column of 3 m (section 0.30 x 0.30 m), loaded to 9 MPa, Kelvin creep
#    (a = 2/E, tau = 60 days) for 300 days: eps = s/E + s a (1 - exp(-t / tau))
m = keta.Model()
c = m.add_material(e=3e10, nu=0.2)
s = m.add_section_props(sx=0.09, iy=0.09**2 / 12, iz=0.09**2 / 12, ix=1e-3)
a_, b_ = m.add_node(0, 0, 0), m.add_node(0, 0, 3)
column = m.add_beam(a_, b_, s, c)
m.fix(a_)
m.add_support(b_, dx=True, dy=True, rx=True, ry=True, rz=True)
m.add_point_load(b_, fz=-0.09 * 9e6)
law = dict(kind="elastic", E=3e10, creep=dict(a=[2 / 3e10], tau=[60.0]))
r = m.fiber_frame({column: dict(rect=(0.3, 0.3, 2, 2), law=law)},
                  stages=[dict(activate=[column], case=1, duration=300.0, time_steps=6)])
eps = r["node_disp"][b_][2] / 3
print(f"fibres: strain at 300 days = {eps * 1e6:.3f} µm/m; "
      f"closed form {-9e6 / 3e10 * (1 + 2 * (1 - math.exp(-300 / 60))) * 1e6:.3f} µm/m")
print("dates of the trace [days]:", [round(t, 1) for t in r["time"]])
Output
stage 1: rotation at x = 2 m: 0.200000 rad, deflection -199.355 mm
stage 2: rotation at x = 4 m: 0.200000 rad, deflection -596.694 mm
fibres: strain at 300 days = -895.957 µm/m; closed form -895.957 µm/m
dates of the trace [days]: [0.0, 0.0, 50.0, 100.0, 150.0, 200.0, 250.0, 300.0]
