Appendix B Formats of the results

The results are ordinary Python types. The table gives the format of the main values; the components follow the order (x, y, z) and (dx, dy, dz, rx, ry, rz).

ValueFormatAxes, units
displacement of a node(dx, dy, dz, rx, ry, rz)global; m, rad
reactions[(node, (Fx, Fy, Fz, Mx, My, Mz)), …]global; N, N·m
sum of reactions(Fx, Fy, Fz)global; N
reactions of skew supports[(node, (6 local), (6 global)), …]support and global
internal forces at the output sections[(x/L, N, Vy, Vz, T, My, Mz), …]local; N, N·m
end forces[Fx, Fy, Fz, Mx, My, Mz] × 2 (origin, end)local; actions of the nodes on the bar
link force(6 components)axes of the link
continuum element stresses(σx, σy, τxy, σz), (σr, σz, σθ, τrz) or (σx, σy, σz, τxy, τyz, τzx)Pa
mode shape or buckling mode[(node, (6 components)), …]global, maximum amplitude 1
participation, effective masses[(6 values, one per direction), …], one row per modeeffective masses: kg, kg·m²; participation: square root of these units (φᵀMφ = 1)
cable at equilibrium(H, V1, V2, Tmax, L0)N, m
tendon at the end of a stage(id, stressed, losses, effective)N
extreme of a vehicle(max, min, position of max, position of min)unit of the response, m
rainflow cycle(range, mean, count)unit of the series
