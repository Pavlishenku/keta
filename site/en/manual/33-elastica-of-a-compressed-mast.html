Chapter 33 Elastica of a compressed mast

33.1 Statement#

A steel mast of height L = 2.5 m, with a 20 mm square section (E = 210 GPa), is fixed at its foot and free at its top. It is compressed at its top by a vertical force F, of fixed direction, greater than its critical load. For forces that tilt the top by 30°, 60°, 90° and 120° from the vertical, the rotation and the displacements of the top are sought.

33.2 Model#

The mast is divided into 80 bars and analysed in large displacements (nonlinear, corotational formulation, Chapter 16) in 50 load increments. A perfectly straight mast would remain on the straight fundamental branch: the geometry receives an imperfection in the shape of the first mode, 1−cos(πz/2L), with an amplitude of L/100,000 at the top (apply_imperfection_shape). A separate unit load case is used to compute the linear critical load.

33.3 Script#

Exampleexamples/manual/app_elastica.py
# Elastica: mast fixed at its foot, free at its top, compressed beyond its critical load.
import math
import keta

E, a, L, n = 210e9, 0.02, 2.5, 80          # steel, square section a x a, height, number of bars
EI = E * a**4 / 12
Fcr = math.pi**2 * EI / (4 * L**2)

def integrals(p):
    """Complete elliptic integrals K(p) and E(p) (arithmetic-geometric mean)."""
    x, y, s, power = 1.0, math.sqrt(1 - p * p), p * p / 2, 1.0
    while x - y > 1e-15:
        c = (x - y) / 2
        x, y = (x + y) / 2, math.sqrt(x * y)
        s += power * c * c
        power *= 2
    K = math.pi / (2 * x)
    return K, K * (1 - s)

m = keta.Model()
sec = m.add_section_props(sx=a * a, iy=a**4 / 12, iz=a**4 / 12, ix=0.141 * a**4)
steel = m.add_material(e=E, nu=0.3)
nd = [m.add_node(0, 0, L * i / n) for i in range(n + 1)]
for i in range(n):
    m.add_beam(nd[i], nd[i + 1], sec, steel)
m.fix(nd[0])
for x in nd[1:]:
    m.add_support(x, dy=True, rx=True, rz=True)              # bending in the XZ plane
shape = [(1 - math.cos(math.pi * i / (2 * n)), 0, 0, 0, 0, 0) for i in range(n + 1)]   # mode 1
m.add_point_load(nd[-1], fz=-1.0, case=2)            # case 2: unit load, for the buckling analysis
Fk = m.buckling(case=2, n_modes=1).factors[0]
print(f"critical load: keta {Fk:.2f} N, pi2 EI / 4 L2 = {Fcr:.2f} N")
print("angle  F / Fcr   angle keta    dX keta   dX exact    dZ keta   dZ exact")
diffs = []
for alpha in (30, 60, 90, 120):                              # target angle of the top [degrees]
    p = math.sin(math.radians(alpha) / 2)
    K, Ep = integrals(p)
    k = K / L                                                # k L = K(p), with k = sqrt(F / EI)
    F = EI * k * k
    mi = m.apply_imperfection_shape(shape, nd, scale=L * 1e-5)   # trigger: L / 100 000 at the top
    mi.add_point_load(nd[-1], fz=-F, case=1)
    ux, _, uz, _, ry, _ = mi.nonlinear(case=1, substeps=50, max_iterations=200).displacement(nd[-1])
    dX, dZ = 2 * p / k, 2 * Ep / k - 2 * L
    print(f"{alpha:4d} °  {F / Fcr:7.4f}  {math.degrees(ry):8.3f} °  {ux:8.4f} m  {dX:8.4f} m  "
          f"{uz:8.4f} m  {dZ:8.4f} m")
    diffs += [abs(math.degrees(ry) / alpha - 1), abs(ux / dX - 1), abs(uz / dZ - 1)]
print(f"maximum relative difference: {100 * max(diffs):.3f} %")
assert max(diffs) < 1e-3

33.4 Results#

The elastica equation, EIθ′′+Fsinθ=0 (θ angle of the tangent with the vertical), is integrated with the complete elliptic integrals K and E of modulus p=sin(α/2), α being the angle of the top. With k=F/EI​:

kL=K(p),dX​=k2p​,dZ​=k2E(p)​−2L(33.1)

dX is the horizontal displacement of the top and dZ its vertical displacement (negative). The force that produces the angle α is F=EIK(p)2/L2, that is F/Fcr​=(2K(p)/π)2; the script computes K and E by the arithmetic-geometric mean. A force 15 % above the critical load already tilts the top by 60°; at 120°, the top is only 0.31 m above the foot.

The angle and the displacements computed by keta differ from these by 0.03 % at most; this difference, which grows with the curvature, is the discretisation error of 80 bars. The linear critical load of the same model, 1,105.40 N, coincides with π2EI/4L2.
