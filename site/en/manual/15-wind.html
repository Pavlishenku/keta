Chapter 15 Wind

15.1 Wind profile#

Syntax
p = keta.WindProfile(vb=26.0, z0=0.05, zmin=2.0, rho=1.225, co=1.0, kI=1.0)
p.vm(z), p.iv(z), p.qp(z), p.kr()
ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
vbm/s26basic wind velocity
z0m0.05roughness length of the terrain
zminm2minimum height
rhokg/m³1.225air density
co—1orography factor
kI—1turbulence factor

Logarithmic profile of EN 1991-1-4: mean wind velocity vm(z), turbulence intensity Iv(z), peak velocity pressure qp(z) [Pa] and terrain factor kr = 0.19 (z₀ / 0.05)0.07.

15.2 Aerodynamic functions#

Syntax
keta.wind_spectrum(form, component, n, U, sigma, L)  →  (m/s)²/Hz
keta.davenport_coherence(n, dy, dz, Uj, Uk, Cy, Cz)
keta.theodorsen(K), keta.sears(K)  →  complex
keta.admittance(kind, K)  →  χ²
keta.scanlan_from_theodorsen(a, K)  →  [(K, H1…H4, A1…A4), …]
keta.vortex_critical_speed(natural_freq, width, strouhal=0.18)  →  m/s
keta.scruton_number(mass_per_length, damping_ratio, rho, width)
keta.vortex_amplitude_ratio(strouhal, scruton, K=0.13, Kw=0.6, clat=0.7)

wind_spectrum gives the one-sided power spectral density of component 0 (u), 1 (v) or 2 (w) for the form 'EN1991' or 'von_karman', with integral σ². admittance accepts 'quasi_steady', 'sears', 'liepmann' or a table [(K, χ²)]. The vortex shedding functions follow EN 1991-1-4 (critical velocity, Scruton number, amplitude relative to the width).

15.3 Aerodynamic description of a deck#

The model computations receive a dictionary spec that describes the wind and the aerodynamic section of the bars:

ParameterUnitDefaultMeaning
vb, z0, zmin, rho, co, kI—profileparameters of the profile (Section 15.1)
spectrum—'EN1991'form of the turbulence spectrum: 'EN1991' or 'von_karman'

15.4 Model computations#

Syntax
m.add_mean_wind_case(spec, case=1)
m.buffeting(spec, requests, n_modes=6)  →  dict
m.temporal_buffeting(spec, requests, n_modes=6, n_max=5.0, n_freq=1024, seed=1, periods=2)  →  (dt, [dict])
m.lrc_peak_case(spec, target, n_modes=6, case=1)  →  (sigma, g)
m.wind_loop(spec, target, permanent_case=None, n_modes=6, max_iterations=20, tol=1e-6)  →  dict
m.flutter(half_chord, a=0.0, n_modes=6, wind_dir=(1, 0, 0), u_min=1.0, u_max=150.0, n_sweep=80,
          zeta=0.0, scanlan=None, rho=1.225)  →  (found, U, ω)

add_mean_wind_case adds to a load case the forces of the mean wind (drag, lift and moment at the mean angle of attack, pressure ρU(z)²/2). buffeting computes the response to turbulent wind by the spectral method (components u and w, coherence, admittance, cross terms between modes) for the points requests = [(node, component)]; it returns dict(responses, modal_cov), responses giving for each point a dict(sigma, sigma_b, sigma_r, nu, g): total standard deviation, quasi-static and resonant parts, up-crossing frequency and peak factor. temporal_buffeting performs the same computation in the time domain (synthesised wind, seed seed). lrc_peak_case adds to a case the equivalent static load (LRC method) that produces the peak value g·σ of the target response. wind_loop chains statics, modes about the loaded state and turbulent response until convergence. flutter searches for the multimodal flutter velocity on the modes of the model (Theodorsen flat plate of half-chord b, axis at a half-chords from the mid-chord, or Scanlan derivatives).

Two functions give the equivalent loads of several correlated effects:

Syntax
keta.lrc_loads(cov, influence, g)  →  [loads]
keta.equiprobable_polyhedron(mean, cov, g)  →  [vertices]
Exampleexamples/manual/ex_vent.py
# Wind: EN 1991-1-4 profile, mean wind, turbulent response (spectral and time-domain),
# equivalent static load and flutter of a 60 m deck.
import math
import keta

p = keta.WindProfile(vb=26.0, z0=0.05)
for z in (10.0, 30.0, 60.0):
    print(f"z = {z:4.0f} m: vm = {p.vm(z):6.3f} m/s, Iv = {p.iv(z):.4f}, qp = {p.qp(z):7.1f} Pa")
print(f"critical vortex-shedding speed (n = 1.2 Hz, d = 2.5 m, St = 0.18): "
      f"{keta.vortex_critical_speed(1.2, 2.5):.3f} m/s")
print(f"Scruton number (m = 12 t/m, zeta = 0.5 %): {keta.scruton_number(12e3, 0.005, 1.225, 2.5):.2f}")
c = keta.theodorsen(0.1)
print(f"Theodorsen function C(0.1) = {c.real:.4f} {c.imag:+.4f} i")

# Deck 12 m wide, 60 m span, 30 m high, axis along Y, wind along X
m = keta.Model()
concrete = m.add_material(e=35e9, nu=0.2, rho=2400.0)
sec = m.add_section_props(sx=6.0, iy=2.0, iz=60.0, ix=5.0)
nd = [m.add_node(0, 5.0 * i, 30) for i in range(13)]
for i in range(12):
    m.add_beam(nd[i], nd[i + 1], sec, concrete)
m.add_support(nd[0], dx=True, dy=True, dz=True, ry=True)
m.add_support(nd[-1], dx=True, dz=True, ry=True)
mid = nd[6]
spec = dict(vb=28.0, B=12.0, zeta=0.01,
            aero=dict(alpha=[-0.5, 0.5], cd=[0.6, 0.6], cl=[-1.5, 1.7], cm=[-0.1, 0.12]))
m.add_mean_wind_case(spec, case=1)
print(f"mean wind: vertical deflection at midspan {m.analyze().case(1).displacement(mid)[2] * 1e3:.3f} mm")
rep = m.buffeting(spec, [(mid, 2)], n_modes=3)["responses"][0]
print(f"turbulence (spectral): sigma = {rep['sigma'] * 1e3:.3f} mm (quasi-static {rep['sigma_b'] * 1e3:.3f}, "
      f"resonant {rep['sigma_r'] * 1e3:.3f}), peak factor g = {rep['g']:.3f}")
sigma, g = m.lrc_peak_case(spec, (mid, 2), n_modes=3, case=2)
print(f"equivalent static load (case 2): deflection {m.analyze().case(2).displacement(mid)[2] * 1e3:.3f} mm "
      f"= g sigma = {g * sigma * 1e3:.3f} mm")
dt, res = m.temporal_buffeting(spec, [(mid, 2)], n_modes=3, n_max=12.0, n_freq=2048, seed=1)
print(f"turbulence (time domain, seed 1): step {dt:.4f} s, sigma = {res[0]['sigma'] * 1e3:.3f} mm")
# Flutter: deck flexible in torsion (J = 0.06 m4), 120 m, 24 bars
m = keta.Model()
concrete = m.add_material(e=35e9, nu=0.2, rho=2500.0)
sec = m.add_section_props(sx=6.0, iy=1.2, iz=60.0, ix=0.06)
nd = [m.add_node(0, 5.0 * i, 30) for i in range(25)]
for i in range(24):
    m.add_beam(nd[i], nd[i + 1], sec, concrete)
m.add_support(nd[0], dx=True, dy=True, dz=True, ry=True)
m.add_support(nd[-1], dx=True, dz=True, ry=True)
print("natural frequencies [Hz]:", [round(f, 4) for f in m.modal(n_modes=4).frequencies])
ok, U, w = m.flutter(half_chord=6.0, a=-0.1, n_modes=2, u_min=5, u_max=400, n_sweep=120)
print(f"flutter (Theodorsen flat plate): {'found' if ok else 'absent'}, "
      f"U = {U:.3f} m/s, f = {w / (2 * math.pi):.4f} Hz")
