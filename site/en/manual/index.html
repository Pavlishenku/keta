Application examples

This part gathers eleven classical problems of structural mechanics, each treated by a short script: beams, frames and trusses in linear statics, among them a fractal truss, thermal actions, a beam on elastic soil, buckling, large displacements and natural frequencies. Each problem has an analytical solution, derived or recalled in the text; the script computes it beside the keta result, prints the relative difference and checks the agreement with an assertion. The outputs reproduced are those of the execution of the scripts.
